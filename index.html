Writing

Three walls I hit running local models (and what fixed them)

Running image and video models on my own rig taught me three rules nobody documented.

1. Krea2 (the Qwen-Image family) does not want tag soup. My SDXL habits failed there. It wants one descriptive paragraph: subject, then outfit, then pose, then scene, then light, then camera. And cfg 1.0 means the negative prompt is dead weight, so you can only say what you DO want. "Not nude" has to be written as what is worn.

2. Wan2.2 image-to-video melts a hand pressed against a hard surface. My red-car test: a hand on the fender came back as a white blob. Hands on hips, holding a hem edge, or free are safe. Avoid a palm or elbow leaning on anything. Add a camera instruction every single time, even "static", or it drifts.

3. fp8 weights on a 16 GB card thrash unless ComfyUI starts with --disable-pinned-memory. That one flag turned a stalled box into a working one.

None of these are settings I read somewhere. They are scars. Writing them down so the next person doesn't pay for them twice.
